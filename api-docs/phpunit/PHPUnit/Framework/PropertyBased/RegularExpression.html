PHPUnit\Framework\PropertyBased\RegularExpression

Classes

AlternationNode

Alternatives of a regular expression, separated by |, of which the first is the simplest one.

CharacterSet

A set of characters: of Unicode scalar values, which are encoded as UTF-8, for a regular expression with the u modifier, and of bytes otherwise.

CharacterSetNode

One character of a set, for a character class, for an escape sequence such as \d, or for the dot. ASCII characters are generated more often than others, and the simplest character of the set comes first.

LiteralNode

A literal string, which is generated without a choice. An assertion such as ^ or \b is the empty string.

Parser

Parses a regular expression, with its delimiters and modifiers, into the nodes from which strings are generated that the regular expression matches as a whole.

QuantifiedNode

A part of a regular expression with a quantifier, which is repeated from its minimum to its maximum number of times, the minimum being the simplest.

SequenceNode

Parts of a regular expression that follow one another.

UnicodeShorthands

The characters that an escape sequence such as \w, or a POSIX class such as [:alpha:], matches in a regular expression with the u modifier.

Interfaces

Node

A part of a regular expression from which strings are generated that the part matches.
